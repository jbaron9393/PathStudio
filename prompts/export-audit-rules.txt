You are performing PASS 2, a cloze-quality audit of an already edited Anki note.

You receive BOTH the ORIGINAL card and its PASS 1 edit. The ORIGINAL is the source of truth for medical information; PASS 1 is the working draft. Use the original to prevent factual drift, lost useful information, altered values/relationships, or unsupported additions. Do not silently regenerate medicine from memory.

PASS 1 has already cleaned and organized the note. Do not redo that work. Preserve its useful organization and wording when faithful to the original. Make only tiny wording changes when required to place a cloze naturally or restore source meaning. Do not expand the card or add medical facts.

OBJECTIVE
Maximize information per cloze—not cloze count and not cloze removal. Ask: “What are the 1–4 pieces of information this card was actually created to make the learner recall?” The final hidden portions should be those facts, with visible information supporting them.

AUDIT EACH CLOZE
1. Identify the exact medical fact it tests.
2. Delete it if its answer is mostly grammatical, predictable, redundant, or low value.
3. Move it to a nearby higher-information medical term when the wrong portion is hidden.
4. Keep it only if it is among the card’s most valuable retrieval targets.
5. Combine targets under one c-number only when they are naturally one recall task. Never group an unrelated qualifier such as “moderate” with an ApoE mechanism.
6. Add a missing high-yield cloze when the card is essentially untestable or an obvious defining target was left visible.

TESTABILITY FLOOR
- A successful Cloze note must retain meaningful hidden information.
- If there are zero meaningful clozes, select the single highest-information target and cloze it; add one or two more only when clearly valuable.
- Do not automatically cloze the first noun or heading. The entity/diagnosis is often an excellent c1 when the rest of the card describes it, but do not force an entity cloze when the existing question already supplies the clue and directly tests another answer.
- “Be conservative” means fewer high-information clozes, not the fewest possible clozes.

TARGET DENSITY (SOFT GUIDANCE)
- Short card: usually 1–2 excellent logical groups.
- Medium card: usually 2–3 excellent logical groups.
- Long card: usually 2–4, occasionally 5.
- Entire sections may remain visible. Never add groups merely to cover sections or bullets.
- Longer cards get MORE REASONING, not MORE HIDING. A long card may appropriately leave most of its text visible.

FINAL CLOZE-SIZE AUDIT (HIGHEST PRIORITY AFTER SEMANTIC EDITING)
- After every other Pass 2 decision is complete, inspect EVERY individual {{cN::...}} span. This is mandatory even when Pass 1 created the span.
- Cloze the smallest meaningful recall unit. Prefer 1–2 meaningful words per individual span; accept 3 words only when they form a meaningful medical unit.
- For EVERY span longer than 3 words, stop and internally identify the specific semantic reason all words must be recalled together. If there is no strong reason, shrink the wrapper to the highest-information 1–3 word target and move all explanation, context, consequences, and other list items outside it.
- Rare inseparable exceptions may exceed 3 words, such as {{c1::anion gap metabolic acidosis}}. Do not split a genuine medical term, molecule, translocation, equation, or other inseparable unit merely to satisfy a mechanical count.
- Word count applies to each individual span, not the total hidden by a shared c-number. When facts belong to one recall task, use multiple small spans with the same number rather than one large wrapper.
- A full sentence, explanatory clause, long list, or multi-step mechanism should almost never be one cloze. Presume paragraph- and section-sized clozes are wrong.
- Ask of every span: “What is the smallest piece of this hidden text that demonstrates the medical knowledge being tested?” Then ask: “Am I hiding explanation or context that would make recall faster?” Shrink and expose context whenever either answer requires it.
- Never solve under-clozing by wrapping an entire answer block in one cloze. Stop editing once every cloze is concise, meaningful, and high-information.

- Prefer the smallest meaningful medical unit, not blindly one word.
- Good: ApoE {{c2::E2}}; AR mutation in {{c2::ABCA1}}; {{c2::orange tonsils}}; {{c2::microvesicular steatosis}}; {{c2::EWSR1-WT1}}; {{c2::Desmin}}+ (dot-like).

PARTIAL-WORD CLOZES
- Keep a partial-word cloze only when the fragment itself is the useful directional distinction: {{c1::hypo}}calcemia, {{c1::hyper}}kalemia, {{c1::under}}estimates, {{c1::over}}estimates.
